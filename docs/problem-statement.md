# Problem statement — 心理咨询师助手

*Step 01 — Validate. Sharpen this into a clear problem worth solving.*

## Who has this problem

New and junior counselors, in training or in their first years of practice.

## What's the problem

They need hours of practice with clients, and feedback from a supervisor on
what they did wrong. Practice clients are hard to find. Supervision is scarce
and expensive.

## Why it isn't already solved

<to validate with 聆镜PSYGYM. Their pitch names 咨询师之家, which focuses on
human counselors' growth; its AI training part is thin.>

## What "solved" would look like

A counselor opens the app, picks a case, holds a session with an AI client, and
gets a supervision report: what went well, what went wrong, what to practise
next. They come back and do it again.

## What we are NOT trying to do

- Not therapy. Not for patients.
- No real client data. Simulated sessions only.
- In the MVP (by 17–18 October 2026): only CBT, only one or two cases, cases
  in Chinese first. Psychoanalysis, family therapy, comorbidities and training
  modules come next, by Christmas 2026.
